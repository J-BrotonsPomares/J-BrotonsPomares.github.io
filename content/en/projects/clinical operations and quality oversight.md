### Simulated Trial Context & Design
* **Protocol ID & Design:** `ARI-DM2-201` (*AURORA-DM2*) — Simulated Phase IIb, multicentre (12 simulated sites in Spain), randomized (`1:1:1`), double-blind, placebo-controlled trial evaluating two once-daily oral doses of *ARI-201 / Aritemida* (25 mg and 75 mg QD) vs. placebo added to stable metformin over 24 weeks plus a 4-week follow-up (`n = 180` simulated participants).
* **Regulatory Frameworks Applied:** ICH-GCP E6(R3) (*Quality by Design* and Critical-to-Quality `CtQ` factors), ICH M11 template structure, ICH E9(R1) estimands (*Treatment Policy*), ALCOA+ data integrity principles, and EU CTR 536/2014 (*Serious Breach* assessment under Art. 52).

### Deliverables Breakdown (Expandable by Module)

<details>
  <summary><strong>Part I — Protocol, Governance &amp; CtQ Risk Matrix</strong></summary>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Governance &amp; Traceability:</strong> <em>Assumption Register</em> and <em>Open Issues Log</em> to track operational assumptions.</li>
    <li><strong>Protocol Operational Summary:</strong> Structured under ICH M11 and ICH E9(R1) (<em>Treatment Policy</em> estimand strategy).</li>
    <li><strong>Schedule &amp; Risks:</strong> <em>Schedule of Assessments</em> with visit windows and Early Termination (<code>ET</code>) visit, plus a 5 × 5 Critical-to-Quality (<code>CtQ</code>) Risk Matrix.</li>
  </ul>
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Part II — Operational Plans (RBM, ALCOA+ &amp; Deviation Governance)</strong></summary>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Risk-Based Monitoring (RBM):</strong> Integrated strategy combining <em>Targeted SDV</em> (with tapering rule), <em>Source Data Review (SDR)</em>, and <em>Centralized Monitoring</em>.</li>
    <li><strong>Data Integrity &amp; Compliance:</strong> ALCOA+ controls, deviation classification/escalation, and regulatory <em>Serious Breach</em> assessment under Art. 52 of EU CTR 536/2014.</li>
  </ul>
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Part III — Simulated Site Evidence (Feasibility, SIV, ISF &amp; CAPA)</strong></summary>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Site Feasibility:</strong> Comparative scoring of 3 simulated sites (<code>Site A / B / C</code>) using a 1–5 rubric, confidence rating, and 3-scenario sensitivity analysis.</li>
    <li><strong>SIV &amp; Query Management:</strong> <em>Site Initiation Visit (SIV)</em> readiness/follow-up tracking and <em>Query Tracker</em> management with query aging metrics.</li>
    <li><strong>Audit Readiness &amp; CAPA:</strong> <em>Investigator Site File (ISF)</em> audit-readiness review, 5-Whys Root Cause Analysis, CAPA plan with effectiveness checks, and STAR technical defense.</li>
  </ul>
</details>
